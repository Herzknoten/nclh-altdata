# Stage-1 model-labelled results

October 2, 2026 semantic analysis of Arctic Shift Reddit records covering January 2025 through September 2026.
Units are hypothesis contrasts, community-period tone summaries, topic rates and model-rater agreement groups.
`hypotheses.csv` reports `analysis_set`, `claim`, `estimate_points`, sampling bounds, classification bounds and `verdict`.
`final_verdicts.csv` reports `protocol_verdict`, failed prerequisites and `release_eligible`; `tone_cells.csv` supplies tone summaries.
`registered_primary/` holds the primary stage-1 analysis; `combined_diagnostic/` adds pilot records under its declared weighting rules.
`combined_union_H1_sensitivity/` is a separate sensitivity analysis; common authors receive one final period vote across waves.
Model-only sampling intervals remain separate from classification uncertainty; model-rater agreement is not evidence of accuracy.
Human validation is absent, all release gates remain closed, and these results do not establish passenger satisfaction.
