# Pilot model-labelled diagnostics

Pilot semantic analysis of the January 2025 through September 2026 Arctic Shift Reddit corpus, retained with the October 2026 study.
Units are hypothesis contrasts, community-period tone cells, topic rates and model-rater agreement groups.
`hypotheses.csv` contains `claim`, `estimate_points`, sampling bounds, classification bounds, `n_authors` and `verdict`.
`tone_cells.csv` contains `community`, `period`, `negative_share` and `balance_points`; `agreement.csv` measures repeatability.
Period estimates give each observed eligible author one vote after sampling adjustment and compare communities with their own baselines.
Root-thread resampling supplies conditional sampling intervals; separate raters expose sensitivity to model labels.
`decision_sheet.csv` and `foundational_gates.csv` retain failed prerequisites; absent human labels leave full classification uncertainty.
All primary verdicts remain model-labelled and not estimable; model agreement does not validate passenger-satisfaction claims.
