# Raw year-over-year cross-check
An unreviewed August-September comparison of 2027 and 2026 price ratios within the same lead band.
`raw_aug_sep_same_band_output.txt` is the only retained result.
It reports Norwegian, Royal Caribbean and Carnival totals and separate inside, oceanview, balcony and suite rows.
Fields are product counts, median 2027/2026 ratios and sailing counts for each year.
The output reports median product ratios, but does not retain the product definition or matching procedure.
The original review brief labels this cross-check unreviewed.
The extract date, source export and exact lead band are not recorded in this folder, so comparability remains unverified.
