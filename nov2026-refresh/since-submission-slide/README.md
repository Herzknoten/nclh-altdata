# Since-submission slide data

November 2026 comparison tables combining All Aboard observations through November 4 and NCLH Q2/Q3 filings.
The main units are departure-cohort groups, sailing windows and company quarters; each CSV retains its source fields.
`P1_early_cuts.csv` reports equal-month cut shares; `P2_latest_shares.csv` reports `n_cut`, `n_sailings` and `share_cut_pct`.
`P3_onboard_gap.csv` divides revenue by passenger cruise days before calculating year-over-year growth.
`C4_q3_print.csv` compares guidance and actuals with explicit row units; `street.csv` contains separately dated market observations.
Older `C1`, `C2`, `C3` and `watch_*` files are retained supporting tables, not inputs to the revised main panels.
