# Slide 13 chart data and images

All Aboard price evidence through November 4, 2026, prepared as three panels and matching CSVs.
`L13_cut_early_by_line.csv` groups sailing-cabin units by `line`, `departure_month` and `lead_days`.
`R13_closein_discipline.csv` reports rolling-window `gap_pp`, brand cut shares and sailing counts.
`B13_the_prize.csv` reports equal-month re-cut shares by `line` and `lead_band`.
Early cuts mean 10% below first ask by T-420; close-in cuts mean a net 5% fall in a matched cabin basket.
Re-cuts mean a further 10% fall within 90 days after the first 5% cut; retained counts show differing coverage.
