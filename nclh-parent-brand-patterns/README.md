# Pricing patterns across parent companies
This folder compares asking-price patterns across brands and ships within NCLH and competing cruise groups.
Source: the September 14, 2026 All Aboard export, with observations through September 12, 2026.
Units are sailing-cabin histories, brand-day cut shares and ship summaries, with all regions primary and Caribbean sensitivity checks.
`out/summary.csv` records `parent`, `brand`, `present_signatures`, `strength`, counts and `main_caveat`.
`out/correlation_summary.csv` records shared-date support; `out/opening_summary.csv` includes `below_control_units` and `below_control_share`.
Comparisons match embark port, exact voyage length and cabin; group correlations also require common brands, dates, products and weights.
The corrected group comparison had eight common dates, below its 30-date floor, so within-parent versus across-parent statistics are unavailable.
Norwegian's primary opening comparison had 33 matched units, with five first quotes more than 5% below different-sailing controls.
A lower first quote is not a same-sailing price cut, and observed price patterns do not establish system adoption or management intent.
