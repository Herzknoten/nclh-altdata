# Regional and cabin-class projections
Archived revenue, capacity and price-path projections with charts by cruise brand, region and cabin class.
The tables cover monthly cohorts from July 2026 through December 2027; the source export and calculation vintage are not documented here.
`revenue_by_cohort_by_region.csv` contains Cohort, Region, Ships, Voyages, cabin revenues and a total index based at September 2026 = 100.
`total_revenue_by_cohort.csv` includes Status, Passengers, Passenger-nights, Weighted avg PPPN and revenue reconciliation columns.
Its stated calculation multiplies price per person per night by passengers and average nights; estimated and modeled rows are labeled.
`capacity_days_by_month.csv` separates listed, missing and expected capacity; `unscraped_fleet_bottom_up.csv` records ship-month gap assumptions.
`brochure_vs_scrape_summary.csv` and `brochure_vs_scrape_sailings.csv` compare brochure coverage with scraped voyages.
Regional subfolders and root PNG files preserve the corresponding charts.
These are model outputs rather than reported revenue; complete methodology and source lineage remain unverified.
