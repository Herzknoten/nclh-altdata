Unsupported indicator quarters remain blank; future close-in signals are not imputed. A method-quarter with any negative allocation is excluded from the current range as physically invalid, while its raw diagnostics remain available. Ranges are min/max method scenarios, not confidence intervals. Min/max envelopes splice methods and need not themselves aggregate to quarterly guidance; each complete underlying method does.
