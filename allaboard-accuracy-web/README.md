# All Aboard methodology research
Provenance note for research on the accuracy and representativeness of the All Aboard asking-price panel.
The original research and claim verification were prepared on 2026-09-14.
Sources included All Aboard Deals, All Aboard Analytics, cruise-line announcements and travel-agency policy pages.
The original work compared provider statements and checked quoted claims against source pages.
Its unit was a documented claim with a source URL and access date, rather than a sailing or price observation.
No tabular data or original research notes are retained in this folder.
The research distinguished advertised fares from realized yield and found that historical price inclusions remained unverified.
