# Markdown evidence scorecard
This folder compares pricing changes consistent with a strategy reset, weak demand, or both.
Source: the September 14, 2026 All Aboard export, using observations through September 12, 2026.
Units are sailing-cabin histories and matched comparison cells for Norwegian, Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Celebrity and Princess.
`out/matched_metrics.csv` contains `metric`, `common_strata`, `target_value`, `peer_value`, `gap` and sample counts.
`out/matched_breadth.csv` measures cut breadth and concentration; `out/scorecard.csv` records interpretations and caveats.
Comparisons match product and calendar support, with Caribbean embarkations primary and all-region sensitivities.
Cut episodes, later price changes and concentration are evaluated with promotion and leave-one-ship-out sensitivities.
The corrected scorecard reports 31 neither-distinguished, 15 unavailable and six overlapping-evidence entries.
Asking prices cannot identify management intent, bookings, inventory exhaustion or eventual realized yields.
